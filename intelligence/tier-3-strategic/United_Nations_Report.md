## Executive Summary

The United Nations provides a unique international perspective on sanctions implementation, conflict finance, organised crime, corruption and global security threats.

Unlike regulators or financial institutions, the United Nations focuses on how financial systems interact with geopolitical instability, armed conflict, sanctions programmes and transnational criminal networks.

United Nations publications frequently assess:

- International sanctions
- Terrorist financing
- Conflict finance
- Organised crime
- Corruption
- Illicit trade
- Human trafficking
- Weapons proliferation
- Global security threats

These reports provide valuable insight into how geopolitical and security developments create new financial crime risks and emerging typologies.
