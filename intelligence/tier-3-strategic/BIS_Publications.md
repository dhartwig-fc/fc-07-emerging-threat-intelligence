## Executive Summary

The Bank for International Settlements (BIS) provides strategic insight into the future evolution of the global financial system.

Unlike regulators, law enforcement agencies or industry groups, BIS focuses on systemic financial developments, emerging technologies and future financial infrastructure.

BIS publications frequently examine:

- Digital assets
- Stablecoins
- Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)
- Cross-border payments
- Artificial Intelligence
- Financial system resilience
- Digital identity ecosystems
- Emerging financial technologies

BIS provides a valuable forward-looking perspective on how future financial infrastructure may create new financial crime risks and detection requirements.
