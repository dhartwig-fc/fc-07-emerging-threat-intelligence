## Executive Summary

Geopolitical Monitor provides independent analysis of geopolitical developments, international conflicts, economic disruption and emerging global risks.

Unlike regulators, law enforcement agencies or industry bodies, Geopolitical Monitor focuses on the strategic drivers that may eventually create new financial crime threats.

The publication provides valuable insight into:

- Geopolitical instability
- Economic fragmentation
- Sanctions developments
- Strategic competition
- Supply chain disruption
- Trade realignment
- Energy security
- Emerging global conflicts

For the Emerging Threat Intelligence Library, Geopolitical Monitor serves as an upstream intelligence source that helps explain how geopolitical developments may evolve into future financial crime risks.

## Repository Implications

Geopolitical Monitor reinforces the importance of understanding the drivers behind emerging financial crime threats.

Many future typologies will not originate from financial crime innovation alone.

Instead, they will emerge from:

- Geopolitical instability
- Economic sanctions
- Trade restrictions
- Supply chain disruption
- Technological competition
- Financial system transformation

Understanding these drivers enables earlier identification of future risks and more effective analytical planning.
