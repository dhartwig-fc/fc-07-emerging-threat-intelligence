## Executive Summary

The World Economic Forum (WEF) provides strategic insight into emerging global trends, technological disruption and future risks that may impact governments, financial institutions and society.

Unlike regulators, law enforcement agencies or financial intelligence units, the WEF focuses on the long-term evolution of technology, economics and global systems.

WEF publications frequently examine:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Cybersecurity
- Digital Identity
- Digital Assets
- Emerging Technologies
- Future Workforce Transformation
- Platform Economies
- Global Risk Trends

The WEF is particularly valuable for identifying technologies and societal changes that may create future financial crime threats before they become mainstream regulatory concerns.
