# Intelligence Source Matrix

## Purpose

This document provides a consolidated view of the intelligence sources used within the Emerging Threat Intelligence Library.

The objective is to clarify the role, value and analytical contribution of each source.

This matrix supports consistent intelligence collection, source assessment, threat mapping and future analytics planning.

## Strategic Conclusion

The intelligence source matrix demonstrates that financial crime intelligence should not rely on a single type of source.

Effective horizon scanning requires a layered approach combining:

- Regulatory intelligence
- Criminal intelligence
- Financial intelligence
- Industry guidance
- Sanctions intelligence
- Corruption intelligence
- Geopolitical intelligence
- Technology foresight
- International security intelligence

This creates a more complete understanding of how financial crime threats emerge, evolve and translate into future analytical requirements.
