## Executive Summary

RUSI provides independent research and strategic analysis focused on the intersection of financial crime, illicit finance, sanctions, corruption, national security and geopolitical risk.

Unlike regulatory bodies, RUSI frequently focuses on future risks, emerging criminal methodologies and policy developments before they become formal regulatory priorities.

RUSI publications often provide insight into:

- Future sanctions challenges
- State-sponsored illicit finance
- Kleptocracy and corruption networks
- Trade diversion methodologies
- National security threats
- Emerging financial crime trends
- Geopolitical risk developments

RUSI serves as a valuable horizon-scanning source for identifying future financial crime risks and emerging detection opportunities.
