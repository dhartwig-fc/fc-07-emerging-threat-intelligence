# Intelligence Collection Calendar

## Purpose

This document defines the intelligence collection and review schedule for the Emerging Threat Intelligence Library.

The objective is to ensure intelligence sources are monitored consistently and emerging threats are identified early.

This framework supports horizon scanning, threat assessment, prioritisation and future analytics planning.

---

## Intelligence Collection Framework

| Intelligence Tier | Purpose |
|------------------|----------|
| Tier 1 | Core intelligence sources providing strategic and operational threat visibility |
| Tier 2 | Operational intelligence sources providing supervisory, enforcement and financial intelligence |
| Tier 3 | Strategic intelligence sources providing future risk and horizon scanning capability |

---

Each review should assess:

### New Threats

- New financial crime methodologies
- Emerging typologies
- New sanctions evasion techniques
- New digital asset risks
- New fraud risks

### Threat Changes

- Increased threat activity
- New intelligence indicators
- Regulatory developments
- Criminal adaptation

### Analytics Implications

- New detection opportunities
- New scoring opportunities
- New network analytics opportunities
- New AI use cases

### Repository Impact

- New ET records required
- Existing ET updates required
- Future scenario updates required
- Threat prioritisation changes required

The following events require immediate review regardless of collection schedule.

### Regulatory Triggers

- Significant Wolfsberg publication

### Sanctions Triggers

- Major OFSI advisory

- Significant sanctions enforcement actions

### Criminal Intelligence Triggers

- Egmont typology publication

### Technology Triggers

- Significant AI developments

- New identity technologies

### Geopolitical Triggers

- Major sanctions events
- Significant trade restrictions

The intelligence programme should demonstrate:

- Threat intelligence translated into analytics
- Future risks monitored consistently

## Strategic Objective

The purpose of the collection calendar is to ensure FC-07 remains a living intelligence capability rather than a static repository.

Regular collection and review activities enable the repository to continuously transform intelligence into:

- Emerging threat assessments
- Future scenarios
- Detection opportunities
- AI opportunities
- Analytics roadmaps
- Strategic financial crime insights
