## Executive Summary

FinCEN advisories provide some of the most operationally relevant financial crime intelligence available globally.

Unlike strategic reports, FinCEN publications often contain:

- Real-world case examples
- Typology indicators
- Red flag guidance
- SAR filing expectations
- Criminal methodologies
- Emerging financial crime risks

Recent FinCEN publications continue to highlight significant risks involving:

- Trade-Based Money Laundering
- Sanctions Evasion
- Russian Sanctions Circumvention
- Human Trafficking
- Cybercrime
- Business Email Compromise
- Virtual Assets
- Money Mule Networks
- Fentanyl and Drug Trafficking Networks

FinCEN increasingly emphasises intelligence-led detection and network-based analysis rather than purely transactional monitoring.

## Key Findings

### Trade-Based Money Laundering

FinCEN continues to identify TBML as a major global money laundering threat.

Key concerns include:

- Over invoicing
- Under invoicing
- Multiple invoicing
- Phantom shipping
- Trade diversion
- Shell company involvement
- Third-party payment activity
