## Executive Summary

Europol's EU-SOCTA 2025 identifies a significant evolution in serious and organised crime.

Criminal organisations are increasingly:

- Digital
- Networked
- Technology enabled
- AI enabled
- Globally connected
- Data driven

The report highlights how criminal networks are exploiting artificial intelligence, digital assets, online platforms, synthetic identities and increasingly sophisticated laundering methodologies to support illicit activity.

Europol assesses that organised crime is becoming more adaptable, resilient and difficult to detect using traditional monitoring approaches.
