## Executive Summary

The Wolfsberg Group represents some of the world's largest international financial institutions and provides industry-leading guidance on financial crime risk management.

Unlike FATF, which establishes international standards, Wolfsberg focuses on practical implementation guidance for financial institutions.

Particular areas of relevance include:

- Trade Finance Financial Crime Controls
- Correspondent Banking Risk
- Sanctions Compliance
- Customer Due Diligence
- Beneficial Ownership
- Transaction Monitoring
- Risk-Based Financial Crime Frameworks

Wolfsberg publications often provide practical guidance on how institutions should operationalise controls and investigations.

## Key Findings

### Trade Finance Financial Crime Risk

Wolfsberg identifies trade finance as one of the highest-risk areas for:

- Trade-Based Money Laundering
- Sanctions Evasion
- Fraud
- Proliferation Financing

Key vulnerabilities include:

- Trade document manipulation
- Complex supply chains
- Hidden ownership structures
- Trade diversion
- Third-party involvement
