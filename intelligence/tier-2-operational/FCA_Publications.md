## Executive Summary

The Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) provides supervisory intelligence regarding financial crime controls, governance expectations and regulatory priorities across the UK financial services sector.

Unlike law enforcement agencies, the FCA focuses on how firms design, implement and maintain effective financial crime systems and controls.

Recent FCA publications increasingly focus on:

- Financial crime governance
- Customer Due Diligence (CDD)
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
- Sanctions controls
- Fraud prevention
- Risk assessment frameworks
- Cryptoasset controls
- Artificial Intelligence governance
- Technology-enabled compliance

The FCA continues to emphasise intelligence-led, risk-based financial crime programmes supported by effective governance and ongoing control optimisation.  [oai_citation:0‡FCA Handbook](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook?entityId=fcg&utm_source=chatgpt.com)
