## Executive Summary

The Office of Financial Sanctions Implementation (OFSI) is responsible for implementing, supervising and enforcing UK financial sanctions.

OFSI publications provide valuable insight into:

- Financial sanctions compliance
- Enforcement priorities
- Ownership and control risks
- Reporting obligations
- Licensing frameworks
- Sanctions evasion methodologies

Recent OFSI publications demonstrate increasing focus on:

- Sanctions enforcement
- Ownership transparency
- Russia sanctions
- Trade sanctions compliance
- Digital asset risk
- Cross-border financial crime
- Intelligence-led sanctions monitoring

OFSI increasingly emphasises risk-based sanctions compliance supported by effective governance, ownership analysis and ongoing monitoring.
