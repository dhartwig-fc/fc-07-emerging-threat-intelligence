## Executive Summary

The Egmont Group represents the global network of Financial Intelligence Units (FIUs).

Unlike regulators or industry bodies, the Egmont Group provides insight into what FIUs are observing through Suspicious Activity Reports, financial intelligence investigations and international intelligence sharing.

Egmont publications frequently identify:

- Emerging money laundering methodologies
- Terrorist financing trends
- Cross-border financial crime activity
- Trade-based money laundering indicators
- Financial intelligence best practices
- International cooperation challenges

The Egmont Group provides a unique operational intelligence perspective because its observations originate directly from financial intelligence investigations.
