## Executive Summary

The Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) provides some of the most operationally detailed sanctions intelligence available globally.

Unlike broader regulatory bodies, OFAC publications frequently focus on real-world sanctions evasion methodologies, trade diversion techniques, ownership concealment structures and illicit financial networks.

Recent OFAC advisories increasingly focus on:

- Iranian oil sanctions evasion
- Maritime sanctions circumvention
- Trade diversion
- Shadow fleets
- Front companies
- Beneficial ownership concealment
- Digital asset misuse
- Complex cross-border financial networks

OFAC intelligence provides valuable insight into how sanctioned actors adapt their behaviour to evade financial controls and regulatory restrictions.  [oai_citation:0‡OFAC](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/934236/download?inline=&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Repository Implications

OFAC intelligence reinforces the need for:

- Trade-focused sanctions analytics
- Network intelligence
- Beneficial ownership resolution
- Maritime intelligence
- Digital asset monitoring
- Cross-border risk analytics

OFAC repeatedly demonstrates that sophisticated sanctions evasion relies upon the combination of:

- Trade structures
- Corporate concealment
- Network complexity
- Documentation manipulation
- Alternative payment mechanisms

Traditional screening controls alone are unlikely to identify these activities. Effective future controls increasingly require:

- Graph Analytics
- Entity Resolution
- Trade Route Monitoring
- Maritime Intelligence
- AI-Assisted Investigation
