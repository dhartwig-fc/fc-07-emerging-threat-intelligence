# ET007 – Social Media Mule Recruitment

## Threat Description

Social Media Mule Recruitment involves the use of social media platforms, messaging applications and online communities to recruit individuals to receive, transfer or withdraw criminal funds.

Criminal networks may advertise fake job opportunities, investment schemes, influencer-style income opportunities or quick-money offers to attract money mules.

Recruited individuals may knowingly or unknowingly allow their bank accounts, digital wallets or payment accounts to be used to move illicit funds.

This threat is particularly relevant to fraud, scam proceeds, authorised push payment fraud, cybercrime, drug trafficking and broader money laundering networks.

## Threat Indicators

### Recruitment Indicators

- Customer linked to known mule recruitment campaigns
- References to online job offers or investment schemes
- Unexplained third-party payments
- Customer profile inconsistent with payment activity
- Sudden account activity following social media contact

### Account Indicators

- Newly opened accounts
- Dormant account reactivation
- Multiple inbound payments from unrelated parties
- Rapid onward transfer of received funds
- Funds withdrawn shortly after receipt

### Behavioural Indicators

- High payment velocity
- Multiple small-value inbound payments
- Account used mainly as a pass-through vehicle
- Frequent changes to contact details
- Use of mobile-first digital channels
