# ET010 – Deepfake Identity Fraud

## Threat Description

Deepfake Identity Fraud involves the use of artificial intelligence to create synthetic images, videos, voice recordings and digital personas that imitate legitimate individuals for the purpose of financial crime.

Criminal actors may use deepfake technologies to bypass customer onboarding controls, impersonate customers, manipulate payment authorisation processes and facilitate money laundering, fraud and sanctions evasion.

The increasing accessibility of generative AI tools has significantly lowered the barrier to creating realistic synthetic identities and impersonation attacks, creating substantial challenges for traditional identity verification processes.

## Threat Indicators

### Identity Indicators

- Inconsistencies between submitted documents and customer behaviour
- Synthetic or manipulated facial imagery
- Repeated use of similar identity attributes
- Unusual onboarding patterns
- Verification failures following successful onboarding

### Behavioural Indicators

- High-value transactions shortly after account opening
- Unusual account activity
- Rapid changes to customer details
- Increased transaction velocity
- Activity inconsistent with customer profile

### Authentication Indicators

- Voice authentication anomalies
- Video verification inconsistencies
- Biometric mismatch indicators
- Suspicious device usage
- Remote onboarding anomalies
