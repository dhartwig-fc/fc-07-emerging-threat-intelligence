# ET009 – Supply Chain Finance Manipulation

## Threat Description

Supply Chain Finance Manipulation involves the misuse of supply chain finance, receivables financing, invoice financing and trade finance programmes to facilitate fraud, money laundering, sanctions evasion or the concealment of financial risk.

Criminal networks and fraudulent organisations may exploit supply chain finance structures by creating fictitious invoices, inflating receivables, disguising beneficial ownership or establishing artificial trading relationships.

The complexity of modern global supply chains creates opportunities for financial crime through the manipulation of counterparties, financing arrangements and trade documentation.

## Threat Indicators

### Trade Indicators

- Repeated financing of similar invoices
- Unusual invoice growth patterns
- Trade volumes inconsistent with customer profile
- Significant invoice concentration
- Limited evidence of underlying trade activity

### Counterparty Indicators

- Newly established suppliers
- Shared ownership structures
- Concentrated customer relationships
- Unusual supplier dependency
- Connected trading entities

### Financial Indicators

- Rapid financing utilisation
- Unexplained increases in receivables
- Circular financing arrangements
- Repeated refinancing activity
- Financing inconsistent with historical behaviour
