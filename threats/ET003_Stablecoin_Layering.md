# ET003 – Stablecoin Layering

## Threat Description

Stablecoin Layering refers to the use of fiat-backed digital assets to obscure the movement, ownership and origin of illicit funds.

Criminal networks may exploit stablecoins to move value rapidly across jurisdictions, exchanges and wallets while maintaining a perceived connection to traditional fiat currencies.

Stablecoins increasingly feature within laundering schemes due to their liquidity, global accessibility and ability to facilitate cross-platform transfers outside traditional banking controls.

The growth of stablecoin ecosystems introduces new financial crime risks relating to money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud and cross-border value transfer.

## Threat Indicators

### Transaction Indicators

- Rapid movement between wallets
- Multiple transfers within short periods
- High transaction velocity
- Fragmented transfer patterns
- Repeated movement through intermediary wallets

### Customer Indicators

- Limited source of wealth information
- High-risk customer profile
- Unexplained digital asset activity
- Newly established customer relationships

### Behavioural Indicators

- Conversion between stablecoins and fiat
- Repeated exchange transfers
- Cross-border value movement
- Frequent wallet creation and abandonment
