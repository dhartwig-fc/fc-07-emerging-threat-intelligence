# ET008 – Embedded Finance Abuse

## Threat Description

Embedded Finance Abuse refers to the exploitation of financial services integrated into non-financial platforms, applications and ecosystems to facilitate money laundering, fraud, sanctions evasion and illicit value transfer.

As banking services become increasingly embedded within e-commerce platforms, marketplaces, fintech applications, gig economy services and digital ecosystems, criminals may exploit gaps in onboarding, monitoring and risk management controls.

The complexity of embedded finance models can create fragmented visibility across customers, transactions and counterparties, increasing financial crime risk.

## Threat Indicators

### Customer Indicators

- Rapid onboarding activity
- Multiple accounts linked to the same customer
- Limited customer verification information
- Unusual customer growth patterns
- High-risk customer segments

### Transaction Indicators

- High transaction velocity
- Unusual merchant activity
- Circular payment flows
- Frequent wallet funding and withdrawals
- Cross-platform movement of funds

### Platform Indicators

- Weak onboarding controls
- Limited transaction monitoring capability
- Third-party payment processors
- High concentration of new accounts
- Marketplace settlement anomalies
