# ET005 – Trade Finance Sanctions Evasion

## Threat Description

Trade Finance Sanctions Evasion involves the deliberate use of trade transactions, shipping networks, intermediaries and financial structures to circumvent international sanctions regimes.

Criminal organisations, sanctioned entities and state actors may exploit weaknesses in global trade systems to conceal the true origin, destination, ownership or end use of goods.

These schemes frequently involve complex corporate structures, transshipment hubs, front companies and falsified trade documentation to disguise sanctioned activity.

Trade finance remains one of the highest-risk areas for sanctions evasion due to the volume, complexity and international nature of global trade.

## Threat Indicators

### Trade Indicators

- Unusual trade routes
- Multiple transshipment points
- Inconsistent shipping information
- Significant pricing anomalies
- High-risk commodities

### Customer Indicators

- Newly established trading entities
- Limited business history
- Complex ownership structures
- High-risk jurisdiction exposure
- Unusual trade counterparties

### Documentation Indicators

- Missing documentation
- Altered invoices
- Inconsistent product descriptions
- End-user discrepancies
- False declarations of origin
