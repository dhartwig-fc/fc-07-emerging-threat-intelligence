# ET004 – Crypto Trade Finance Laundering

## Threat Description

Crypto Trade Finance Laundering involves the use of digital assets to facilitate, settle or obscure international trade transactions.

Criminal networks may use cryptocurrencies and stablecoins to settle trade obligations outside traditional banking channels, reducing transparency and bypassing established AML and sanctions controls.

The convergence of digital assets and international trade finance creates new opportunities for Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML), sanctions evasion and cross-border value transfer.

These schemes often combine traditional trade finance structures with digital asset ecosystems, making detection increasingly challenging.

## Threat Indicators

### Trade Indicators

- Trade transactions lacking traditional payment records
- Significant pricing anomalies
- High-risk trade corridors
- Inconsistent trade documentation
- Third-party settlement arrangements

### Digital Asset Indicators

- Stablecoin settlements linked to trade transactions
- Transfers involving high-risk exchanges
- Use of privacy-enhancing services
- Frequent wallet-to-wallet transfers
- Cross-chain transaction activity

### Behavioural Indicators

- Digital asset activity inconsistent with customer profile
- Rapid conversion between crypto and fiat
- Trade payments settled outside established banking relationships
- High transaction velocity
