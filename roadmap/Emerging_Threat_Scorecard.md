# Emerging Threat Scorecard

## Purpose

This document provides a structured framework for assessing, prioritising and monitoring emerging financial crime threats.

The objective is to support strategic decision-making by evaluating threats based on likelihood, impact, intelligence confidence and analytical readiness.

The scorecard translates intelligence into actionable priorities for future analytics, controls and investigation capabilities.

## Strategic Conclusion

The scorecard demonstrates that not all emerging threats require the same level of attention or investment.

Priority should be given to threats where:

- Likelihood is increasing
- Potential impact is significant
- Intelligence confidence is strong
- Existing detection capabilities are limited

Current analysis suggests that the most important future financial crime capabilities are likely to involve:

- Trade and sanctions analytics
- Network intelligence
- Identity intelligence
- Stablecoin analytics
- AI-assisted investigation
- Supply chain intelligence
