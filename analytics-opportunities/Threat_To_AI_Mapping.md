# Threat to AI Mapping

## Purpose

This document identifies how Artificial Intelligence may support future financial crime detection, investigation and risk management.

The objective is to map emerging threats to practical AI-enabled use cases.

## Strategic Conclusion

AI should not replace financial crime judgement.

Its highest-value role is to support investigators by:

- Explaining complex alerts
- Summarising risk factors
- Prioritising work
- Reducing manual research
- Improving consistency
- Supporting decision documentation

This directly links emerging threat intelligence to future AI-enabled investigation capability.
