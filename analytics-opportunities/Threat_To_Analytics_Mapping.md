# Threat to Analytics Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps emerging financial crime threats to the analytical capabilities required to detect, investigate and manage them.

The objective is to identify the analytics building blocks needed for future financial crime controls.

---

## Threat to Analytics Matrix

| Threat ID | Threat | Analytics Required |
|-----------|--------|--------------------|
| ET001 | AI Generated Trade Documents | Document analytics, similarity detection, metadata analysis |
| ET002 | Synthetic Identity Networks | Entity resolution, identity analytics, network analytics |
| ET003 | Stablecoin Layering | Blockchain analytics, wallet attribution, transaction flow analytics |
| ET004 | Crypto Trade Finance Laundering | Trade analytics, blockchain analytics, network intelligence |
| ET005 | Trade Finance Sanctions Evasion | Trade route analytics, ownership analytics, graph analytics |
| ET006 | Open Banking Mule Networks | Payment analytics, behavioural analytics, mule network detection |
| ET007 | Social Media Mule Recruitment | Behavioural analytics, mule account detection, network analytics |
| ET008 | Embedded Finance Abuse | Platform analytics, merchant analytics, ecosystem risk scoring |
| ET009 | Supply Chain Finance Manipulation | Counterparty analytics, invoice analytics, trade network analytics |
| ET010 | Deepfake Identity Fraud | Identity analytics, biometric analytics, behavioural monitoring |

---

## Core Analytics Capabilities

| Capability | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Entity Resolution | Identify linked customers, entities and attributes |
| Graph Analytics | Detect hidden relationships and criminal networks |
| Behavioural Analytics | Identify unusual changes in customer or transaction behaviour |
| Trade Analytics | Detect trade anomalies, pricing issues and route risks |
| Digital Asset Analytics | Monitor wallets, stablecoin flows and blockchain activity |
| Identity Analytics | Detect synthetic identity and onboarding risk |
| AI-Assisted Analytics | Support investigation, summarisation and decisioning |

---

## Strategic Conclusion

Emerging threats increasingly require combined analytics rather than single-rule detection.

Future financial crime analytics will require the integration of:

- Network intelligence
- Behavioural monitoring
- Risk scoring
- Digital asset tracing
- Trade analytics
- AI-assisted investigation
