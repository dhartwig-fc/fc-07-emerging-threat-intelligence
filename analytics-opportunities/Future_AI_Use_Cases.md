# Future AI Use Cases

## Purpose

This document captures potential applications of Artificial Intelligence in financial crime detection, investigation and risk management.

---

## Investigator Copilot Opportunities

### AI-Assisted Alert Summaries

Objective:

Automatically summarise:

- Alert rationale
- Risk indicators
- Related entities
- Historical activity

---

### AI-Assisted Investigation Plans

Generate:

- Investigation checklist
- Missing information requests
- Escalation recommendations

---

### AI-Assisted SAR Drafting

Generate:

- SAR narratives
- Investigation summaries
- Management reporting
