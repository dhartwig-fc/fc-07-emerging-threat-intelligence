
# Threat to Source Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps emerging financial crime threats to the intelligence sources that provide supporting evidence, indicators, typologies and strategic context.

The objective is to ensure that all emerging threats are supported by credible intelligence and can be traced back to recognised regulatory, law enforcement, financial intelligence, sanctions and strategic sources.

This mapping also helps identify intelligence gaps and prioritise future collection activities.

## Strategic Conclusion

Threat-to-source mapping provides evidence that emerging threats should not be assessed using a single intelligence source.

The strongest threat assessments emerge when multiple intelligence disciplines converge, including:

- Regulatory intelligence
- Criminal intelligence
- Financial intelligence
- Sanctions intelligence
- Governance intelligence
- Technology intelligence
- Strategic foresight

This mapping framework enables a more structured and defensible approach to emerging threat prioritisation.
