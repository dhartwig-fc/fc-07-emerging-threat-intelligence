# Threat Prioritisation Framework

## Purpose

This framework supports prioritisation of emerging financial crime threats for future analytics investment.

The objective is to identify which threats should be addressed first based on likelihood, impact, regulatory attention and current detection gaps.

---

## Assessment Criteria

| Criteria | Description | Weight |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Likelihood | Probability of threat materialising or increasing | 30% |
| Impact | Potential financial crime, regulatory or operational impact | 30% |
| Regulatory Attention | Level of focus from regulators, FIUs and law enforcement | 20% |
| Detection Gap | Weakness of current detection capability | 20% |

## Priority Categories

| Priority | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| Critical | Immediate monitoring and future analytics design required |
| High | Include in analytics roadmap and threat monitoring |
| Medium | Monitor through intelligence sources |
| Low | Review periodically |

---

## Strategic Use

This framework helps determine:

- Which threats should be monitored most closely
- Which analytics capabilities should be prioritised
- Which threats require future detection models
- Which areas may require AI-assisted investigation capability

---

## Conclusion

Threat prioritisation ensures that analytics development is driven by risk, intelligence and regulatory relevance rather than technology interest alone.
