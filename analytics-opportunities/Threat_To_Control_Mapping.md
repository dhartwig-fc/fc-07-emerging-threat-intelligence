# Threat to Control Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps emerging financial crime threats to potential control frameworks and mitigation strategies.

The objective is to translate emerging intelligence into practical financial crime control design.

---

## Threat to Control Matrix

| Threat ID | Threat | Control Area | Potential Controls |
|-----------|--------|--------------|--------------------|
| ET001 | AI Generated Trade Documents | Trade Finance Controls | Document validation analytics, metadata review, AI content detection |
| ET002 | Synthetic Identity Networks | Customer Due Diligence | Entity resolution, identity analytics, device intelligence |
| ET003 | Stablecoin Layering | Digital Asset Controls | Wallet monitoring, blockchain analytics, transaction flow analysis |
| ET004 | Crypto Trade Finance Laundering | Trade Finance Controls | Trade-to-wallet correlation, settlement analytics, digital asset screening |
| ET005 | Trade Finance Sanctions Evasion | Sanctions Controls | Trade route monitoring, ownership analytics, sanctions network analysis |
| ET006 | Open Banking Mule Networks | Transaction Monitoring | Payment velocity analytics, mule account scoring, beneficiary monitoring |
| ET007 | Social Media Mule Recruitment | Fraud Controls | Mule risk scoring, behavioural analytics, linked account detection |
| ET008 | Embedded Finance Abuse | Platform Risk Controls | Merchant monitoring, ecosystem risk analytics, platform due diligence |
| ET009 | Supply Chain Finance Manipulation | Trade Controls | Invoice validation, receivables analysis, counterparty risk assessment |
| ET010 | Deepfake Identity Fraud | Identity Controls | Biometric validation, onboarding risk scoring, behavioural monitoring |

---

## Control Themes

Future controls are expected to centre around:

- Identity verification
- Network intelligence
- Trade documentation validation
- Digital asset monitoring
- Behavioural analytics
- AI-assisted investigation
- Sanctions exposure analysis

---

## Conclusion

This mapping supports the movement from threat intelligence to control design.

It ensures each emerging threat can be linked to practical monitoring, investigation or governance responses.
